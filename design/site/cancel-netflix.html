How to cancel Netflix

Checked Nov 10, 2026on Netflix Help· By Aribo· 2 min read

Short answer: go to netflix.com/cancelplan in a browser, tap Cancel, then Finish Cancellation. You can keep watching until the end of the month you paid for. No cancel button? You're billed by a partner, like your phone or TV company. Cancel with them instead.

Netflix website or appBilled by AppleBilled by a partner

Cancel on your iPhone or computer

  1. Open netflix.com/cancelplan in Safari and sign in
  2. Tap Cancel
  3. Tap Finish Cancellation and keep the confirmation email
Netflix says this is the only way to cancel.The Netflix iPhone app doesn't have a cancel button. Use the website, even on your phone.

No cancel button?

Then Netflix isn't billing you directly. Look under Membership on netflix.com/account to see who does: often your phone, internet or TV company. Cancel through them.

Signed up through Apple years ago? New Apple sign-ups ended long ago, but old ones may still bill there. Check Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Have this ready

  • The email you use for NetflixNot sure? Search your inbox for "Netflix" receipts.
  • The last 4 digits of the card it chargesHandy if you have more than one account.

What happens after you cancel

You keep watching until the end of the period you already paid for. Netflix won't charge you again after that.
