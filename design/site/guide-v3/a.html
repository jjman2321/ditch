How to cancel Spotify Premium

Cancel on Spotify’s website. It takes about a minute, and you keep Premium until your next bill.

Open spotify.com/account ↗

On the web

  1. Go to spotify.com/account in Safari and sign in.
  2. Tap Manage your plan.
  3. Tap Cancel subscription, then confirm.

If Apple bills you

Signed up in the iPhone app? Then Apple bills you. Your statement says APPLE.COM/BILL.

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Spotify, then Cancel Subscription.

From support.apple.com

After you cancel

You keep Premium until your next billing date, then it switches to free. Your playlists stay.

From support.spotify.com
