How to cancel Spotify

Pick how you pay. Each way takes about a minute.

On Spotify’s websiteMOST PEOPLE

Manage your plan → Cancel subscription.

Open spotify.com ↗

Through Apple

Statement says APPLE.COM/BILL. Cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Show me how ↓

Through Google Play

Google Play → Subscriptions → Spotify → Cancel.

Show me how ↓

Through your phone company

No cancel option on Spotify? Your carrier bills you. Cancel with them.

What to do ↓

From support.spotify.com, support.apple.com, support.google.com

After you cancel

Until your billYou keep Premium until your next billing date.
Free afterYour account switches to free, with ads.
Playlists stayYour music library stays where it is.
