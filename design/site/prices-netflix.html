Netflix prices in 2026

Checked Nov 9, 2026on Netflix's own help pages· By Aribo

Short answer: in the US, Netflix costs $8.99 a month with ads, $19.99 for Standard and $26.99 for Premium. In the UK it's £7.99, £13.99 and £20.99. US prices are before tax; UK and EU prices include VAT.

Cheapest way to get Netflix

The plan with ads is the cheapest almost everywhere. Netflix's pages for Ireland, New Zealand and the Netherlands list no plan with ads, so Basic is the cheapest there. India also has a phone-only Mobile plan at ₹149.
