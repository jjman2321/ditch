Questions

How many people can be on Spotify Family?

Up to 6 accounts, all at the same address. Duo is for 2.

Is Spotify Family worth it?

Once 2 or more people would pay for Premium anyway, each share costs less than Premium Individual.

Can I split Spotify Family with friends?

Spotify's rules say everyone on the plan lives at the same address.

Who pays for Spotify Family?

One person, the plan manager. Splitting the cost is up to you.
