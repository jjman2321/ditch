A · The stake on top, your reminders below

The plans, button and links are pinned to the bottom and look the same on every phone. The headline is their own yearly total at stake. Below it, the notifications Pro sends about their own subscriptions, with the job right there (How to cancel). One point of view, no feature table. Taller phone = more notifications, and the last one slides under the plans. Never an empty block.

B · How the free week works

Same pinned bottom. The top is the trial timeline (Today → reminder → charge date). On taller phones the steps spread out, with a cap. When Lifetime or Weekly is picked, the steps switch to that plan.
