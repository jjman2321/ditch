# Variation 1 · Receipt

**Concept:** your subscriptions are a paper receipt. Ditch prints it, you stamp what you don't want. Proof over decoration: every number is real and adds up.

- **Fonts:** Bricolage Grotesque 800 (headlines and big numbers), DM Mono (receipt lines and labels), Inter as the stand-in for SF Pro (body).
- **Colours:** warm canvas #F3F1EB, paper white, ink #121212, stamp red #DD3B26, duck-bill orange #F5A524.
- **What makes it ours:** the receipt with a torn edge, the red DITCHED stamp, "hold to ditch", Ditch the duck peeking over the paper, and the paywall that puts "Ditch Pro trial" on your own receipt ("We'll remind you before we charge you, too").
- **Details:** "*** THAT'S $2.99 EVERY DAY ***" receipt footer; the Ditched screen tells you the next step (cancel with Netflix) because Ditch never cancels for you.

**Weakest point:** the stamp and fonts are stand-ins (font plus a roughness filter). The final stamp should be hand-lettered, and the duck needs a professional redraw.
