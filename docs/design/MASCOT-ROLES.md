# The raccoon must make sense on every screen

Owner rule (Nov 9, 2026): **the mascot always makes sense.** On each screen it shows what's happening there, or how to feel about it. It's never decoration.

Look: the premium-white direction. A soft, plush teal raccoon with a cream face, friendly round eye patches (never a bandit band) and a lime scarf. One character model everywhere.

## Rules
- Every pose shows the screen's action or feeling. If you can't say why it's there, remove it.
- It's small on work screens (lists, dates, plans) and bigger only on Welcome, Total and You're set.
- It never covers a button or text, and never moves near a price.
- With Reduce Motion on, show the still first frame of each loop.
- Never in or coming out of a bin (BitePal does that). Never eating. No teeth. Never named "Rocket".
