# Onboarding + paywall playbook

Distilled from ~1,000 screens studied on Mobbin (Nov 9, 2026). Sources:
- `docs/research/duolingo-brilliant.md`
- `docs/research/wellness-mascots.md` (Finch, Ahead, Gentler Streak, Flo, Wysa, Me+)
- `docs/research/bold-mascots.md` (BitePal, Cal AI, Brainly, Cleo, Speak)
- `docs/research/paywalls-competitors.md` (70+ paywalls, plus Orbit, Rocket Money and Bobby)

## Rules every study agreed on
1. **Value before any ask.** The total comes before permissions and payment. We already do this.
2. **Two mascot layouts only:**
   - a small avatar with a speech bubble on question screens, and it doesn't re-animate between screens;
   - a big centred mascot for hellos, reactions and results.
3. **The mascot replies in one short line (4–10 words)** and reacts to what you just did. It answers a skip kindly.
4. **One decision per screen.** The button wording changes with state ("None of these" → "Continue").
5. **Mascot size follows the screen:** big on talk screens, small on work screens, small and calm on the price screen.
6. **Permission:** a pre-ask that shows the user's *own* future reminders. "Maybe later" is a real option. Never draw a fake iOS alert.
7. **Paywall:**
   - The billed amount is the biggest price, and any monthly breakdown is smaller (Apple's rule).
   - The trial timeline uses real dates, with the exact amount in the charge row.
   - Prove any saving on screen ("$4.99 a month · $59.88 a year"). No strike-through prices.
   - × and Restore are visible from the first frame. "Continue free" is a real button.
   - **Let the user pick the reminder day** (Duolingo and Opal). For Ditch this works as a live demo of the product.
   - One page beats several (Superwall/Stompers, 2026).
8. **End on a "You're set" summary** with the first real reminder. A receipt-style "You're in" after purchase (M2).

## Never (all seen live in the studied apps)
- Exit offers, "gift" pop-ups, countdowns.
- Crossed-out prices, per-week breakdowns.
- Trial toggles. No close button.
- Fake "analysing…" loaders.
- Made-up reviews or stats.
- Pledges ("draw a tick", "sign a contract") before the paywall.
- A user-named pet, a personality picker.
- The raccoon living in or popping out of a bin (BitePal's thing).

## Our flow (v3)
| # | Screen | Mascot | Main copy | Button |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome | big, card-flick pose | "Ditch it before it renews." / "I'll add up what you pay and warn you before each charge." | Let's see what I pay |
| 2 | Tick | small avatar, fixed | Bubble reacts: "What do you pay for? Tap them all." → "Nice! What else?" → "4 so far. Keep going!" | None of these → Continue |
| 3 | Reveal | big, shocked with receipt | "That's about $887 a year." / "$73.94 a month across 6 subscriptions." / "Let's make sure none of it surprises you." | Set my dates |
| 4 | Dates | small avatar, fixed | "When does Netflix charge you?" plus a confirmation line after each tap | (tap a day) · Not sure · Later |
| 5 | Reminders | small avatar with bell | "I'll ping you 3 days and 1 day before a renewal. That's all I'll ever send." Lock screen shows their own subs. | Turn on reminders · Maybe later |
| 6 | Paywall | small, calm, bell | "Get a heads-up before all 6 renewals." Logo row FREE/PRO. Plans. Timeline with a reminder-day picker and the "Ditch Pro trial" row. | Start 7-day free trial · Continue free: 3 reminders |
| 7 | Pick 3 | small, cheering | "Free it is! I picked your 3 biggest. Swap any time." | Done |
| 8 | You're set | big, cheering | "You're set." First heads-up date, number tracked, reminders on/off with a fix. | Go to my list |

## Open decisions for the owner
- **The raccoon's name**, for "Hi, I'm ___" on Welcome. It can't be "Rocket".
- **A founder line on the paywall** ("Made by one person. No ads. Your list stays on your phone.") Use it only if true.
- **Mascot art:** the bold-mascot study says our current pose set shares BitePal traits (round eyes inside dark patches, front-on grin). The mascot study's fix: a ¾ view and smaller eyes. Redraw before animating, ideally with a human illustrator.
