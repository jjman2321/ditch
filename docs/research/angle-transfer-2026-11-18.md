## Best transfer: Scroll the Quran
- Proof of mechanic: Scroll The Bible ~$882k/month within a year.
- Gap: Quran versions exist only as tiny clones; none has design quality, trust or scholarship.
- Owner fit: a Muslim founder (authenticity, mosques/MSAs/Ramadan as distribution), and it matches his earlier "ditch doomscrolling + micro-learning" idea.
- Moat: trust and correctness (verified translations/tafsir sources), community, saved ayat and reflections history, Ramadan seasonal content.
- Risks:
  - lower $/download for the mass Muslim audience
  - translation licensing (use public-domain or licensed translations, e.g. Sahih International terms; Quran.com API terms to check)
  - scholarly accuracy (needs a reviewer)
  - **mascot conflict**: many Muslim users avoid animal characters in religious apps, so the duck likely stays out of this app
- Next: owner decides on the mascot question; then a deep dive (Scroll The Bible's exact loop, paywall, reviews; Muslim willingness to pay; Ramadan spikes; licensing).
