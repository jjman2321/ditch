# Full app audit (Nov 19, 2026)

Real app (web preview of the actual code, not mockups), 36 screens × iPhone SE / iPhone 12 / Pro Max + dark mode (144 shots). Four independent reviewers, each benchmarked against Mobbin and reading the code. I re-checked every P1 below against the code or screenshots. Owner-decided items the reviewers questioned are listed at the end and are **not** changes.

## P2: polish
- Slides 3: drop "exactly" ("We show you how to cancel it").
- Where should we look?: on SE "Pick them myself" falls off screen; one-line descriptions; no chevron on the disabled bank row; "FREE" vs Find hub's "FREE ONCE · THEN PRO" (use the honest one).
- Your total: empty gap above the button; add the fact line "Most people guess about half that."
- Reminders pre-ask: lock-screen mock shows a "Fri, Nov 20" card on "Monday, December 14"; second card covers the torch/camera icons.
- Back controls differ (Skip / ‹ Back / chevron / ‹ Home); button label colour flips white/yellow between screens.
- Find review empty state is bare: add a line and the duck.
- Find Apple: "coming in the next update" promises a future feature; say what works instead.
- Home: "in 5 days" disappears on some rows when another row has "/yr"; card filter doesn't change the total; free users can't tap the bell.
- Home empty: "Nothing to watch yet." reads like a streaming joke (pick: "Add one. Ditch keeps watch."); 2nd quick-add row behind the tab bar on SE.
- Mixed currencies: "+ US$33.99" looks like adding currencies (rule 1). One per line, no "+".
- Edit: no hint when the price matches no plan; Save active before any change.
- Add Hulu: Pro lock hides a number the free chip already shows.
- Calendar: a reminder dot sits next to the wrong logo.
- Ditched tab: "+$215.88 a year" (reads like income, cents vs whole dollars); title changes by phone size; buttons wrap on SE; cancelled rows don't look tappable; empty state has no button.
- Cancel help: "Cancel in Netflix" → "Open Netflix to cancel"; step 1 repeats the button; Apple line is a hidden link.
- Ditch screen: big gap on Pro Max. Payday: 7-column grid looks like weekdays. Currency: no "no results" line.
- Paywall in-app: same headline/× label as onboarding; Ditch Pro page (free user) is bare; Which 3 empty bell low contrast in dark mode; "Nov 21 at / 9 AM" wraps on SE.
- Your data: "No account, no bank link." becomes untrue when the bank scan ships; delete must then also remove the bank connection.
- Old RevenueCat comments in purchases.ts / pricing.ts / pro.tsx / PlanCard.tsx (DECISIONS #59).

## Checked and fine
All money math (totals, monthly/yearly, overlap, before-payday, mixed-currency rough line), reminder dates, paywall prices ($0.96/wk, Save 86%, Nov 26 charge), cancel help honesty, red + confirmed delete, VoiceOver labels on rows/cards, tap targets, dark mode, no "saved" anywhere.

## Not changing (owner decisions)
Welcome small print "Free to track. No account needed." (owner, Nov 19) · no trial timeline on the paywall · "Save 86%" without "vs weekly" · Pro row style in Settings.
