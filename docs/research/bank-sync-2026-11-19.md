# Bank connection: scan once or always on? (Nov 19, 2026)

Owner question: do apps make people connect manually each time, or keep an always-on sync so new subscriptions get added automatically? Builds on `launch-vs-autofind-2026-11-18.md` (providers, prices, timeline), `finder-apps-2026-11-18.md` (how MonoBoost, Subpilot and Feenko work) and `docs/plans/plaid-v1.md`. **(est.)** = my model. Not a locked decision until the owner picks.

## How the apps do it
| App | Connection | What happens after |
|---|---|---|
| Rocket Money, Monarch, Copilot (budgeting apps) | **Always on** (Plaid and others) | New charges flow in daily; subscriptions and bills are detected from them; "Inactive" when no charge for a month ([Rocket Money help](https://help.rocketmoney.com/articles/934383-missing-subscriptions)) |
| MonoBoost, Subpilot, Feenko (subscription finders) | **Always on** (Plaid, plus Gmail/Outlook) | "Automatic subscription detection" is the paid feature (MonoBoost: Premium) |
| Bobby, Orbit, Outflow, most small trackers | **Manual** (Outflow also reads Apple FinanceKit on the phone) | You add everything yourself |
| Ditch today (DECISIONS #64 plan) | **Scan once, then disconnect** | Finds what you pay now; nothing new is spotted later |

So every app that sells "we find your subscriptions" keeps the connection on. Scan-once is cheaper and more private, but it can't deliver "new subscriptions show up on their own", which is the owner's selling angle.

## What always-on would give Ditch
1. **New subscriptions appear on their own:** a new repeat charge shows up as "New: Max $16.99 a month. Add it?" One tap to add. (Ask, never add silently: wrong detections are the top complaint about finder apps.)
2. **Real charge checks:** "Netflix charged $17.99 today", which proves the reminders matched reality.
3. **Price rises caught from the real bill**, not only from the price list.
4. **Proof you cancelled:** no charge on the due date → "Looks cancelled. Mark it ditched?"
5. **Retention:** the app keeps earning its place every month. Scan-once apps have a "job done" problem (see `ditch-vs-sober-2026-11-19.md`).

## What it costs
**Plaid bills Transactions per connected bank ("Item") every calendar month (UTC) for as long as the connection exists**, even with no API calls, even when the connection is broken. It stops only when we call `/item/remove` or the user revokes access ([Plaid billing](https://plaid.com/docs/account/billing/), [stop billing](https://support.plaid.com/hc/en-us/articles/18785119002263-How-do-I-stop-billing-for-Plaid-products-I-m-no-longer-using), [when Plaid bills](https://support.plaid.com/hc/en-us/articles/16195012630807-When-and-how-often-does-Plaid-bill-for-product-usage)). The pay-as-you-go price is only shown in the Production form; third parties report about **$0.25–0.50 per bank a month** (unconfirmed; [Activepieces](https://www.activepieces.com/blog/plaid-pricing-2026-cost-per-connection-and-request)). People connect about 1.6 banks.

| Per user (est., $0.30–0.50 per bank, 1.6 banks) | Plaid cost | What Ditch keeps after Apple's 15% |
|---|---|---|
| Free user, one scan then disconnect | $0.48–0.80 once | n/a (it's a hook) |
| **Yearly Pro $49.99, always on** | $5.76–9.60 a year | **$42.49 → ~$33–37 (78–86%)** |
| **Weekly Pro $6.99, always on** | ~$0.48–0.80 a month | **~$25.75 a month → ~$25 (97%)** |
| Lifetime $99.99, always on | $5.76–9.60 **every year, forever** | $85 once → gone in 9–15 years. **So Lifetime stays at 1 scan a year (DECISIONS #65).** |
| Trial that doesn't convert (connected during the 7 days) | $0.48–0.80, more if the trial crosses a month end | 0 |

Plus Supabase ~$25 a month. **At 1,000 Pro users always on (est.): ~$500–800 a month to Plaid against ~$3,500 a month kept from yearly plans.** It pays for itself as long as the connection is removed the moment someone stops paying.

## The rules that keep it profitable and honest
1. **Always on only while someone pays** (yearly or weekly, including the trial). The second a trial is cancelled or Pro lapses, our server removes the bank connection. This uses Apple's App Store Server Notifications (still no RevenueCat). The list stays and reminders drop to 3 (CLAUDE.md rule 6).
2. **Free users get one scan**, removed straight away (the hook in the paywall flow, DECISIONS #66).
3. **Lifetime: 1 scan a year**, as decided. Always-on can come later as an add-on (DECISIONS #6b).
4. **Ask, don't auto-add.** New finds land in a small "New this week" list. Known catalog services with a clear repeat could be one tap.
5. **A "Scan only" switch in Settings** for people who want the connection gone. Privacy-minded users are our audience.
6. **No push server.** The app checks for new charges when it opens and in iOS background refresh, then shows a normal local notification ("Found a new one: Max $16.99"). This keeps CLAUDE.md's "local notifications only".
7. **Don't store transactions.** The server passes them to the phone, and the phone's repeat detector does the finding (as planned).
8. **US and Canada only** (Plaid pay-as-you-go). Everyone else keeps screenshots and statements; Apple FinanceKit (Apple Card, and UK banks in Wallet) is the free, on-phone always-on route for later.

## Downsides to plan for
- **"Reconnect your bank" prompts.** Banks expire logins (Plaid sends `ITEM_LOGIN_REQUIRED` / pending expiration). That needs a calm "Reconnect" card. Broken links are a top complaint in finder apps.
- **More privacy weight:** we hold an encrypted access token for as long as sync is on. The privacy label already changes for any bank use (DECISIONS #64). Plaid's security questionnaire asks about this.
- **Billing traps:** a connection we forget to remove bills forever. That's why removal on lapse is a hard rule, plus a nightly cleanup job.
- **Never claim more than it does:** "Ditch spots new subscriptions from your US or Canadian bank and asks before adding them." Never "automatic for everything" (CLAUDE.md rule 5).

## Pick: "Bank sync" for Pro, one free scan for everyone (8.5/10)
| Option | Selling angle | Cost risk | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scan once for all (current plan) | "Find what you pay now" | Lowest | 7 |
| **Always on for paying users, one scan for free (pick)** | **"New subscriptions show up on their own"** | Low, if removal on lapse is solid | **8.5** |
| Always on for everyone | Same | Free users cost money every month forever | 4 |

## Build order (est.)
1. Plaid Sandbox + Supabase functions: link token, exchange, sync, remove, webhooks (1 week).
2. Connect and scan flow in the app (dev build) → existing review list (1 week).
3. Always-on: background check, "New this week" list, Reconnect card, Scan-only switch (1–1.5 weeks).
4. Lapse handling: App Store Server Notifications → remove connection; nightly cleanup (lands with payments).
5. Privacy policy, label, App Review notes with a Plaid Sandbox login.
