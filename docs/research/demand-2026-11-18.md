# Is there real demand for Ditch? (Nov 18, 2026)

AppKittie revenue/download figures are model estimates (round numbers, miss web billing): read them as ranks. Raw pulls were kept outside the repo. Budget used: AppKittie 105 credits (2,000 → 1,895), DataForSEO $0.031 (→ $0.3526).

## Short answer
- **The problem is real and growing (8/10).** "How to cancel/stop subscriptions" searches roughly doubled in the US in 2 years.
- **Demand for a paid manual tracker is weak and flat (3–4/10).** The category's money goes to Rocket Money (bank link) and hard-paywall "we find/cancel it for you" apps. No manual tracker reliably makes >$10k/month; none near $50k.
- **Demand score 5/10.** Year-1 base case ~$1–2.5k/month net by month 12; bull ~$7–8k; bear <$200.
- **Strongest demand is "find and stop what I'm paying for"**, not "subscription tracker".

## 4. Year-1 scenarios for Ditch
Assumptions: 70% yearly $29.99 / 20% monthly / 10% lifetime; ~$25 net per payer in year 1 after Apple 15%, VAT, refunds; RevenueCat SOSA 2026 download→paid by day 35: freemium 2.1% (top quartile 4.5%), hard paywall 10.7%; only ~17% of new apps reach $1k MRR in 2 years, ~4.6% reach $10k.
| | Bear | Base | Bull |
|---|---|---|---|
| Downloads/month at month 12 | ~500 | ~3,000 | ~8,000 |
| Downloads year 1 | ~4,000 | ~20,000 | ~55,000 |
| Download→paid | 1.5% | 2.7% | 4.5% |
| Year-1 net revenue | ~$1.5k | ~$13k | ~$60k |
| Net per month at month 12 | <$200 | ~$1.9k | ~$7.5k |
| Probability | ~45% | ~40% | ~10–15% |
Odds: >$1k/month net by month 12 ~35%; >$5k/month by month 18 ~10–15% (DECISIONS #18's 40–60% looks too optimistic); >$10k ~5%. SEO: cancel-guide readers convert to installs at ~0.5–2%, so ASO must carry growth.

## 5. Risks and who still wants a standalone app
Risks: built-in tools (iOS 27 Apple Card Recurring with warnings, US only; iPhone Settings; Capital One, Chase, Revolut, Monzo); cheap one-time rivals (Bobby $2.99, Outflow $4.99); "a subscription to manage subscriptions" backlash; merchant reminder laws; AI assistants doing the finding; 1,068 look-alike apps.
Still wants one: non-US users (Rocket Money is US-only; UK searches rising); multi-card/multi-bank people; Apple-billed subscriptions and trials (APPLE.COM/BILL, 48% forget trials); people who won't link a bank.

## 6. Verdict and what to do
Demand 5/10. Lead with honest on-phone finding (DECISIONS #63) and trial/big-renewal alerts as the paid core; aim ASO at "cancel subscriptions" (UK difficulty 10) while keeping "subscription tracker"; push UK/EU. Re-check after ~1,000 downloads: >4% download→paid → raise odds; <1.5% → hard-paywall switch (DECISIONS #24) or treat Ditch as a learning/portfolio project.
