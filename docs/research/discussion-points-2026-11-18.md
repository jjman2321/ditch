# Discussion points (owner, Nov 18)
Ideas we're talking through, **not rules or locked decisions** (owner: "these aren't rules, more like discussions we talk about"). Bring them up when they're relevant; don't treat them as binding.

1. Paywall structure (how many options, which plan is shown first, trial design) seems to move revenue more than price tests do.
2. Weekly may be the new monthly. The owner's source says a weekly plan with a free trial earns far more than one without ($54.50 vs $7.40 per user). We haven't checked that figure.
3. The first session may be the whole game: most trials start on install day.
4. Keep onboarding as short as possible, unless a step buys a personalised first screen or filters for buyers.
5. The app should do the job, not show data about the job. Ditch's job is getting useless subscriptions cancelled. (Owner: "not sure tbh".)
6. Put the stake on screen, not the stat: people act on what they're about to lose. (Owner, Nov 18: not as the paywall headline.)
7. One point of view, then cut: piling on features and references can make an app worse.
8. Test the paywall often. Top apps run about 15 paywall tests a year.
