# Ditch (subscription tracker) vs the sober app: which has more upside? (Nov 19, 2026)

Builds on `quit-decision-2026-11-18.md` (sober market), `monetization-model-2026-11-18.md` and `keywords-competitors-2026-11-18.md` (Ditch market). AppKittie revenue numbers are its estimates (App Store only, last month). **(Est.)** = my model. Not a locked decision.

## Short answer
**Ditch first, sober app second. Score: Ditch 7.5/10, sober app 7/10.** They're closer than they look:
- **Ditch wins on:** time to revenue (≈90% built), organic search (big, high-intent keywords plus the 192-page SEO site already live), and exit comparables (Truebill sold for $1.275B).
- **The sober app wins on:** retention and moat (a 400-day streak is hard to leave), and a market that's growing as drinking falls.
- **Ditch's biggest risk:** "job done" churn. People clean up their subs, then cancel the tracker. Plus Apple and the banks giving subscription tools away free.
- **The sober app's biggest risk:** paid ads are how leaders grow (recovery forums ban promotion), it's a heavy build (pond, art, 365 days of content), and alcohol-safety responsibility.

## Why Ditch first
1. **It's nearly done.** Shipping it teaches the whole Apple pipeline and brings real data (conversion, retention) for both apps.
2. **Organic is already built.** The website's price and cancel pages are the cheapest growth engine either app has.
3. **Bigger exit comparables**, if the bank scan backend exists (that's what buyers pay for).

## What would flip it to the sober app
- Ditch's day-60 retention or yearly renewal comes in much worse than the category (the "job done" churn is real).
- Apple ships renewal reminders in iOS.
- A 2-week demand test for the sober app beats Ditch's launch numbers.

## Plan
1. Launch Ditch (payments, bank scan, App Store) and watch 60–90 days of real numbers.
2. Meanwhile, run the sober app's 2-week demand test (waitlist pages), as `quit-decision` says.
3. Build the sober app as app #2, reusing Ditch's reminder engine, paywall and duck.
