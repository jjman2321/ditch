# A duck for the app icon? (Nov 9, 2026)

Owner idea: a duck as the app icon only, with a very minimal app built around the DITCHED stamp. The app itself stays character-free (DECISIONS #5).

Icon landscape (competitors and duck apps): `design/icon-landscape.jpg`.

## What a duck means here
| For | Against |
|---|---|
| **Fits the name.** A ditch is a water channel, and ducks live in ditches. | **Daak** already exists: a finance app with a minimal line-drawn duck on white, Apple-featured, 358 US ratings. A minimal duck on white would look like a copy. |
| **"Duck" = dodge.** Duck the renewal. Ditch it or duck it. | **4Ducks** (budget and bills app) uses "get your ducks in a row" and a duck-head dollar sign. Tiny (3 ratings) but same category. |
| **"Get your ducks in a row"** = organised. That's what tracking does. | **DuckDuckGo** owns "duck" in many heads (privacy). Good vibe for "your list stays on your phone", but they defend their marks (they opposed DuckDB's trademark at the TTAB). Stay far from an orange circle and a duck head with a bow tie. |
| **A simple shape.** Reads at 29pt in Settings and in search results. Animal icons stand out in a grid of abstract finance marks (Bobby's squirrel). | **Can read as silly or kids-y** (rubber duck, "sitting duck"). It needs a calm, grown-up drawing. |
| **No BitePal clash** (the raccoon problem is gone). | **Evidence is mixed.** Icon tests show 3–30% swings either way. No study says characters reliably beat symbols. |

## Bobby precedent
Bobby's squirrel is only on the icon. None of its 6 App Store screenshots show it. Icon = brand memory, app = clean. That's the same split the owner wants.

## Scores (for Ditch's success)
| Icon | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Duck, drawn to be ours**: side profile on teal, sitting on a water line (the "ditch"), bold flat shapes, hand-finished vector | **7.5** | Stands out, means something three ways, warm in the one place a character can't sit near money. Risk: Daak and DuckDuckGo look-alikes if drawn lazily. |
| DITCHED stamp | 7 | Fully ours and matches the in-app moment, but colder and wordy at icon size. |
| Minimal line duck on white | 4 | Looks like Daak. |
| Rubber duck | 4 | Toy/kids feel. Wrong for a money app. |

## Pick
**Go with a duck icon, drawn to be distinct, and keep the app minimal with the stamp.** Rules for the drawing:
1. Teal background (our accent), not white and not orange.
2. Side profile, calm, no teeth, no bow tie, nothing round-orange (DuckDuckGo).
3. The water line is the "ditch", so the name and the picture tell one story.
4. Flat vector, finished by hand, not an AI 3D render (trust, Tim Gabe).
5. Test it at 29, 40 and 60pt next to the icons in `design/icon-landscape.jpg`.
6. After launch, test it against the stamp icon with App Store Product Page Optimization.
7. Before filing a design trademark, have a lawyer do a quick check against DuckDuckGo, Daak and 4Ducks.
