# Finding your subscriptions: every honest on-phone route, moats, and bank sync re-checked (Nov 16, 2026)

Builds on `autosync.md` (B1–B9), `platform-moats-2026-11-15.md`, `whats-next-2026-11-15.md`. Owner decision Nov 16: "find my subscriptions" is the launch moat (DECISIONS #63). **(Unverified)** = one weak source or none.

## Summary
1. **Biggest idea: other companies already did the detecting; let the user screenshot their lists.** Apple Card → Recurring (iOS 27, US), Capital One / Chase / Revolut / Monzo / Barclays subscription or recurring pages, PayPal automatic payments, Amazon Memberships & Subscriptions, Google Play subscriptions. Same on-phone reader as the Apple screenshot. Honest ("you show us the list"), cheap, label unchanged.
2. **"Share this page" from Safari:** `expo-share-intent` 9.0.0 `preprocessorInjectJS` can hand Ditch the page text of a signed-in account page. Exact text, no misreading. Needs a 1-day test (size limits unverified).
3. **Free-trial detection from screenshots** is the best moat + money feature: Subpilot sells a "Free Trial Detector" for $39.99–69.99; Apple's screen prints the trial end date.
4. **APPLE.COM/BILL matcher** (match Apple statement lines to the Apple screenshot / Purchase History by amount ± tax and date ± 2 days) is the hardest moat to copy; rivals' reviews: "could not identify Apple transactions".
5. **Clean files:** Apple Card CSV/OFX/QFX/QBO, PayPal activity CSV (`Type` column marks preapproved and subscription payments; full value list unverified), EU CAMT.053.
6. **Impossible (never promise):** reading other apps' notifications, other apps' App Store subscriptions, any API for Apple Card/Wallet recurring charges.
7. **Still skip:** Gmail (CASA audit ambiguity for on-device; Google hasn't confirmed), Outlook (few users), Calendar, Google Takeout, Visual Intelligence.
8. **Bank sync (Plaid etc.): don't launch with it; at most a v3 paid add-on (5/10).** Loses "Data Not Collected" for the whole app, needs a server, UK/EU providers are sales-only or closed (GoCardless/Nordigen closed to signups), ~$45–60/month per 100 paying US users. FinanceKit first (free, on-phone, US Apple Card + 13 UK banks).

## Bank sync re-checked
- **Plaid:** price unpublished (~$0.25–0.30 per Item/month per third parties; Recurring add-on extra); `/transactions/recurring/get` US/CA only; server required (never store access token on the client).
- **Stripe Financial Connections:** $0.30 per institution per account holder per month, US only, no recurring detection, server needed.
- **Teller:** $0.30/enrollment/month, no recurring API, no RN SDK.
- **MX, Finicity, Akoya, TrueLayer, Tink, Yapily, Enable Banking:** sales-led quotes. **GoCardless/Nordigen:** closed to new signups. **SimpleFIN:** user pays $15/yr; clunky.
- **Privacy label:** would become at least "Data Linked to You: Financial Info" for the whole app.
- **Reviews:** Rocket Money (recent 2.2★, ~150 "won't link my bank"), Copilot ("Amex sync broken for months"), Monarch ("broken connections is hell"). Users blame the app.
- **Money:** bank-linked rivals earn more mainly because of weekly plans and hard paywalls (Hiatus, bank link at normal prices: ~$11.7k/mo).
- **Cost (US, 1.5 banks/user):** 100 payers ~$45–60/mo; 1,000 ~$450–600; 10,000 ~$4,500–6,000; server ~$5–50.
- **Verdict:** launch no (2/10); v2 no; v3 separate paid "Auto-sync" (DECISIONS #6b) 5/10, only with 500+ payers, user demand, provider eligibility confirmed, and the owner accepting the label change.
