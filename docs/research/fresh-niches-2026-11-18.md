# Fresh niches: fast new winners outside the quit/habit space (Nov 18, 2026)

Builds on `open-ideas`, `niches-round2`, `pivot-niches`, `demand` and the quit reports from today (none of those niches are repeated here).
**Method.**
1. Apple's free US top-grossing and top-free charts (top 100) for 23 app categories and 15 game sub-genres. Kept only apps released since Jan 1, 2025. That gave about 600 new apps already in the charts.
2. Grouped them into clusters. A cluster only counts if **at least 2 new apps earn money**.
3. Checked each cluster with AppKittie (revenue and downloads, App Store keyword scores), one DataForSEO call (Google US), App Store 1–3★ reviews and web search.

**All AppKittie revenue and download numbers are model estimates** of App Store gross per month. Read them as rankings, not bank statements.
**Budget:** AppKittie 223 credits (log 897 → 1,120, cap 1,147). DataForSEO $0.015.

## 1. Short answer
- **The clearest "Tipple pattern" is the AI budget meal planner.** Three apps launched in **June–July 2026** are already at ~$228k–$601k a month (est.): **mise** (UK), **TapCook** (US) and **Potto** (a solo developer). The recipe is the same in all three: your supermarket + your weekly budget → a week of dinners and one priced grocery list.
- **Their reviews are a gift.** 69–94% of their latest reviews are 1–3★, and the complaints are the ones Ditch already solves:
  - hard paywall before you see a single plan
  - $9.99 a **week**
  - charged after cancelling the trial
  - "prices aren't real"
  - "AI slop, same meals every week"
- **This is the best fit we've found for the owner's target user**: money-anxious, wants control, hates being tricked. An honest paywall is a real advantage here, not decoration. **8.0/10.**
- **Runner-ups:**
  - **Book tracker with a duck reading buddy** (7.5): cheap to build, fully on the phone, three 2025–26 indie apps earn $35–66k.
  - **Car-screen widgets** (6.5): iOS 26 added widgets to CarPlay, and 5 apps launched since Nov 2025 earn $14–471k.
  - **Cozy duck-pond collecting game** (6.5): the duck is the product.
- **Big searches, small money:**
  - "Imposter game" is searched 301k times a month on Google (+1,092%), but it's saturated (485 apps) and earns ~$0.27 per download.
  - "Guess the song" gets 90.5k searches (+224%), but the top new app makes ~$35k and there's music-licensing risk.
- **My pick: the honest budget meal planner**, called something like "Ditch the 6pm panic". Start with a fixed, human-checked recipe library and only a light AI step, so cost and quality stay under control.

## 4. Niche cards (year-1 = net monthly revenue at month 12, after Apple's 15%)

**1. Honest budget meal planner (8.0)**
- **What it is:** Pick your store, your budget and who you're feeding. You get a week of dinners with a grocery list priced at your store. Swap any meal. Tick off what's already in the pantry. Pin "tonight's dinner" as a widget. A 4:30pm reminder says what's for dinner.
- **The opening:**
  - Three apps reached ~$228–601k a month within 5 months, with no well-known brand.
  - Every one of them makes you pay before you see a single plan, and 69–94% of their recent reviews are 1–3★: "$10 a WEEK", "charged after I cancelled", "meals listed at $9 aren't $9", "same salmon and sweet potato every week".
  - Google interest is flat, so this wave is ad-driven. People in reviews say "saw it on TikTok".
  - **Why now:** grocery prices and a fresh wave of ads have proven people will pay, and nobody honest has shown up yet.
- **Duck angle:** the duck as a calm sous-chef. Example copy: "Dinner's sorted. $61 this week." The money line comes from the yearly total reveal Ditch already has ("You'd spend $2,900 a year on takeout").
- **Price:**
  - Free: your first full week plus 3 dinners a week, forever.
  - Pro: $5.99/mo · $29.99/yr. No weekly plan. Uses the trial reminder Ditch already has.
- **Build:**
  - About 6–8 weeks with Claude.
  - Recipes: start with a library of ~300 human-checked recipes plus cost-per-serving rules. Use the AI only for swaps and "use what I have". That's cheaper and gets around the "AI slop" complaint.
  - **Small server needed:** a Cloudflare Worker to call the AI. Expect ~$10–50 a month at a few thousand users.
  - Store prices: Kroger's developer API gives store-level prices (check its terms). Instacart's shoppable-list API pays a commission on orders (Instacart's last published rate was 3%; check current terms).
  - **Privacy label:** sending preferences to an AI provider may move the label from "Data Not Collected" to "Not Linked to You". Re-check this before building.
- **Risks:**
  - Paid ads may be needed to grow (the leaders run them).
  - Fast followers; the clones have already started (Whipp, NeatEat, Herbi).
  - Prices have to be shown as estimates.
  - It sits next to the excluded "takeout" idea, but this is a different product: planning dinners, not tracking takeout.
- **Year 1:** bear $500 · base ~$6k · bull ~$40k.

**2. Book tracker with a duck reading buddy (7.5)**
- **What it is:** Scan a barcode to add a book. A cute shelf. Reading sessions, a yearly goal and stats. A "currently reading" widget. Notes and quotes.
- **The opening:**
  - "book tracker" has App Store popularity 86 with low difficulty (29).
  - New paywalled trackers out-earn the old ones (Shelfy ~$66k vs 2018–21 apps ~$9–10k).
  - But Shelfy's and SlowRead's reviews are 70–75% 1–3★: "paywall before one book", "charged on day 1".
  - Freemium Bookology gets only 16% 1–3★. An honest free tier wins the reviews.
- **Duck angle:** the duck reads next to you during sessions, and DITCHED becomes "DNF: ditched it" (Did Not Finish, with no guilt). That's a real reader in-joke.
- **Price:** free unlimited shelf. Pro $3.99/mo · $24.99/yr · $39.99 lifetime (stats, widget themes, iCloud backup).
- **Build:** 3–4 weeks. Local SQLite, Open Library/Google Books lookups (free), no server.
- **Risks:** Goodreads and StoryGraph are free. TikTok creators drive installs.
- **Year 1:** bear $200 · base ~$3k · bull ~$15k.

**3. Car-screen widgets (6.5)**
- **What it is:** Since iOS 26, any iPhone widget can sit on the CarPlay screen. These apps add photos, a fuel and trip card, countdowns, lyrics, a duck that rides along.
- **The opening:**
  - Five apps launched since Nov 2025 earn ~$14–471k.
  - Their reviews are 85–92% 1–3★: "widgets won't load", "charged after cancelling", "can't cancel".
  - Google "carplay widgets" is up +1,141% (still small at 4.4k).
- **Duck angle:** a little dashboard co-pilot. Fun, but the "Ditch" name doesn't fit.
- **Price:** $2.99/mo · $14.99/yr.
- **Build:** 4–6 weeks. Needs a native WidgetKit extension (Expo config plugin) and a car to test in.
- **Risks:**
  - Apple could ship this itself.
  - myCar's revenue is down 49% over 90 days, so the fad may be cooling.
  - Apple review may reject it as too thin ("minimal functionality").
- **Year 1:** bear $300 · base ~$4k · bull ~$30k.

**4. Cozy duck-pond collecting game (6.5)**
- **What it is:** Decorate a pond. Different ducks visit depending on what you put out (the Neko Atsume loop). Postcards from rare ducks.
- **The opening:**
  - Cozy Florist ~$297k. Cozy Cat Tree is up +1,361% in 30 days.
  - Players complain about long forced ads, lost saves and stacked "events".
  - "cozy games" Google +9%.
- **Duck angle:** best of all nine; the duck is the whole game.
- **Engine:** Expo + Skia + Reanimated is enough for a 2D idle game.
- **Money:** IAP only (cosmetics, a "Pond Pass") keeps "Data Not Collected". Ad SDKs earn more but break that label.
- **Build:** 8–12 weeks. **Art is the bottleneck:** budget $1–3k for an illustrator.
- **Risks:** hit-driven; App Store search is weak ("cozy games" popularity 29).
- **Year 1:** bear $100 · base ~$2k · bull ~$25k.

**5. Daily trivia or puzzle with a mascot (6.0)**
- **What it is:** One short daily round that everyone plays, a shareable result card, streak-free (to stay out of the habit space).
- **The opening:** Farout (Sep 30, 2026) grew +624% with a clever twist: rare answers score more. Its reviews complain that valid answers get rejected, so the answer-checking is the weak spot. Minipuzzles ~$44k.
- **Duck angle:** the duck hosts.
- **Price:** $3.99/mo · $29.99/yr, or a $4.99 one-time "all past days" unlock.
- **Build:** 3–4 weeks, plus a daily content pipeline (AI drafts, human checks). Needs a small server for "how others answered" (~$5/mo).
- **Year 1:** bear $100 · base ~$1.5k · bull ~$15k.

**6. Guess-the-song (5.5)**
- **The opening:** a huge, fast-growing search (90.5k, +224%). Songless's reviews are 94% 1–3★: three songs, then $10/week.
- **Why only 5.5:**
  - Apple's music preview clips are licensed for promoting the music, so a game built on them is a grey area.
  - Spotify shut down Heardle in 2023.
  - Earns ~$0.2–0.3 per download.
- **Year 1:** base ~$1k.

**7. Recipe saver (5.5)**
- **What it is:** Paste a TikTok or Instagram link and get a clean recipe.
- **Why only 5.5:** ReciMe already makes ~$2.07M a month, Google demand is falling, and scraping social posts breaks often.
- **Better as a feature of #1.**
- **Year 1:** base ~$1k.

**8. Pass-the-phone party game (5.5)**
- **The opening:** enormous search interest (imposter game 301k), easy build (2 weeks).
- **Why only 5.5:** 485 lookalikes. The 97k-rating leader earns only ~$85k. The fad is aging.
- **How to try it:** a cheap side project (a "Duck Duck Imposter" word pack), not a business.
- **Year 1:** base ~$800.

**9. Collection tracker (5.5)**
- **What it is:** Track your LEGO sets (or toys, vinyl) with a scanner.
- **The opening:** Brickify ~$107k with an immediate-paywall backlash. Rebrickable's API is free.
- **Why only 5.5:**
  - Small search demand ("lego collection" popularity 14).
  - LEGO trademark care needed.
  - The AI "scan and value" apps (VinylSnap, ToyWorth) come from one app factory and need AI image calls.
- **Year 1:** base ~$1.5k.

## 5. Caveats
- Revenue figures are AppKittie model estimates. Third-party estimates for mise range from ~$300k to $700k a month.
- Meal-planner revenue may churn fast: most of it is likely annual plans bought straight from a trial after an ad.
- The ≤3★ shares come from the 50–200 most recent US (and UK for mise and Potto) reviews, which skew negative right after a paywall change.
- Year-1 ranges assume a solo launch with organic growth plus some TikTok posting and no paid ads. With paid ads, cost per install is roughly $1.5–4.
- Reddit was not checked (blocked here).
