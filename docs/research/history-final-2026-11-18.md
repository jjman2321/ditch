# History app: final research and verdict (Nov 18, 2026)

The owner asked for "every aspect" and had three open questions: (1) is a gamified Duolingo-style path right for history, (2) the name, (3) the niche as a whole.
Builds on `history-app-2026-11-18.md` (Paladin teardown, first 30 lessons, pricing, gate), `aso-seo-history-vs-trading-2026-11-18.md` (keywords, SEO, site plan) and `skill-pick-2026-11-18.md`. Nothing from those is repeated unless a number is needed.
**Budget:** AppKittie **79 credits** (log 1,743 → 1,822, cap 1,823): 6 keywords (4 non-English, 2 US) = 60, 4 country splits = 4, 3 non-English app lists = 15. A Brazil keyword call was rejected (country not supported) and not charged. DataForSEO not used. Everything else was free (iTunes Search, RDAP, TikTok profile pages, cached Wikipedia data, web search).
**AppKittie revenue figures are model estimates.** They miss web sales and can be ~10× off for small apps, so read them as rankings. **Every name below needs a lawyer's trademark search before money is spent on it.**

## 1. Short answers
1. **Format: yes to gamification, no to a Duolingo path that gates you.**
   - Build a **story-first daily lesson** (drawn cards, the duck narrating) with an **optional** 3-question recall, a **daily guess-the-year game**, a **collectible card** per story, and a **streak that any daily action keeps alive, listening included**.
   - The path map only *shows* progress. It never locks a lesson behind a quiz.
   - Why: every history app earning over $100k a month is story-first (Paladin, Imprint, Headway, Nibble, Sophia). Quiz-first and path-style history apps earn $1–20k. ~22 of Paladin's 80 negative reviews are about forced quizzes.
2. **Name: "Oddbit"** (store title "Oddbit: Learn History").
   - Coined, two syllables, easy to say and spell. No App Store app has it. **oddbit.app and getoddbit.com look free; @oddbitapp is free on TikTok.** It grows past history ("the odd bits of history, science, money").
   - **Duckumentary isn't bad (6/10), but it isn't the best.** It's long and easy to misspell, the .com has been taken since 2003, it's already the title of a PBS film and a Donald Duck gag, and it locks you into duck + documentary.
   - **Ditch doesn't work (2/10).** It means "get rid of", a registered DITCH mark exists, 5+ App Store apps are called Ditch, and it would blur with the owner's own subscription app.
3. **Niche: real money from English-speaking men aged 25–44, one weak leader, open non-English markets, a mild seasonal cycle.**
   - Paladin's users are 72% US and **91% in four English-speaking countries**, and the app is English-only.
   - In German and Spanish, no history-learning app earns more than ~$2.4k a month.
   - Interest peaks Nov–Mar and dips Aug–Sep. January is the best month to sell trials.
4. **Verdict: 7/10, GO behind the 30-shorts gate.** v1: 60 stories, 90 daily puzzles, 60 cards, Listen mode, a hard paywall after story 1. iOS first.

- **Duolingo's own non-language courses:**
  - Chess passed **1M daily users while still in beta** (iOS, English only, Q2 2025) and was called "the fastest-growing subject".
  - Math and Music have "**single-digit millions**" of daily users (Q2 2026 call), out of 58.7M in total. The Q1 2026 letter says it's "too early" to see the new subjects in the metrics.
  - So the path works for skills you *practise* (chess, math). Duolingo has picked no knowledge subject like history.
- **Brilliant** (~$260k/mo, $119.99–191.99/yr) proves the path model too, but its lessons are problems you solve. History has little "doing", so our "doing" is putting events in order, guessing years and reading maps.

### 2.2 The Paladin tension (its 100 latest US reviews)
- ~22 of the 80 reviews at 1–3★ hate forced quizzes or ask to "just let me listen". 32 say the app is "nothing like the ads".
- The 4–5★ reviewers like that it replaces doomscrolling, and some say "the quizzes reinforce what I learned".
- **Both sides are right.** Recall practice does improve memory (the testing effect, Roediger & Karpicke 2006). Forcing it is what annoys people.
- So the quiz is **a carrot, not a gate**: it earns XP and a star on the card, and skipping it still counts for the streak.

### 2.3 Retention benchmarks
- **Education apps:** Day 1 ~14–27%, Day 7 ~10–18%, Day 30 ~2–8%. Vendor blogs disagree; they cite AppsFlyer and Adjust. Adjust's average for all apps is ~26% on Day 1 and ~5–6% on Day 30.
- **Streaks work (Duolingo blog):** learners who reach a 7-day streak are **2.4× more likely to come back the next day** (a correlation). Streak changes raised learners on 7+ day streaks by **over 40%**; separating the daily goal from the streak gave **+19%**. Duolingo's returning-user rate (CURR) hit **84%** in Q2 2026, and a Streak Revival campaign brought back 15.4M learners.
- **Mascots: no rigorous retention study.** The only number is second-hand: Duo in notifications added about 5% daily users.
  - The proven value of a mascot is **distribution**: Duolingo's TikTok has ~16–17M followers built on Duo.
  - Simple-cartoon channels lead history on YouTube (OverSimplified 9.7M, History Matters 1.9M).

### 2.4 Recommended format (one sitting, 3–5 minutes)
1. **Today's Story:** 6–10 drawn cards narrated by the duck. They auto-advance like a Short; tap to skip ahead.
2. **Quick recall (optional):** 3 questions → XP + a star on the card. Never a gate. Question types: put it in order (already in the mockup), guess the year, which one is the myth.
3. **Card drop:** the story's collectible ("Julius Quacksar") joins your set, with 1–3 stars.
4. **Daily game:** one guess-the-year puzzle with a share grid. Free forever, and also on the web.
5. **Streak:** any one of story, listen or daily game keeps it. One free freeze a week, one repair after a lapse.
6. **Path map:** seasons and eras shown as progress, never as a lock.
7. **Listen mode (Pro):** a season as a lock-screen playlist. A fully listened story counts for the streak.

### 2.5 Game mechanics: 5 to use, 5 to avoid
| Use | Why |
|---|---|
| **Streak earned by any daily action** | The strongest proven lever (2.4×, +40%). Counting listening fixes Paladin's #3 complaint |
| **Collectible cards with stars** | Completing a set is a reason to return. Shareable, and cheap: one duck plus props, no AI art |
| **Daily guess-the-year + share grid** | The Wordle/MapTap loop: free virality, a web SEO page, a habit trigger |
| **Seasons on a path map** | Visible progress and an end point. It names the TikTok series |
| **Optional recall earning XP and stars** | Keeps the memory benefit without forcing it |

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| **Hearts/lives** | Punishes mistakes and exists to sell Pro (Paladin's "infinite hearts"). The anxious target user hates it |
| **Quiz gates** | ~22% of Paladin's negative reviews |
| **Leagues at launch** | Needs a server and enough players, and adds pressure. Revisit in v2 if Day 30 is under 5% |
| **Boss battles, 1v1** | Server and other players needed. Stress, not learning |
| **Guilt notifications** | Clashes with the dry, on-your-side voice. One helpful reminder at the user's chosen time instead |

- **Paladin's Meta ad reach (AppKittie, Meta transparency):** 25–34 is 39%, 35–44 26%, 18–24 13%, 45+ ~22%; **65% men**.

### 4.2 Seasonality
- **Wikipedia, 30 history articles, Nov 2025–Oct 2026 (index, 1.0 = average):** Nov **1.16** · Jan **1.15** · Mar **1.17** · Jun 0.91 · Jul 0.93 · Aug **0.83** · Sep **0.81**.
  - Interest dips at back-to-school, so school doesn't drive adult curiosity.
- **January is the selling month** (Adapty 2026, all subscription apps): trials surge on Jan 1, first payments land around Jan 10, and install → paid runs **39.4% in mid-January vs 30.5% in October**. Language-app installs rose 26% on Jan 1, 2024 (Statista).
- **Calendar hooks:** Jan resolutions · **Feb Black History Month** (Google: these searches peak every February) · **Mar Women's History Month** · May 8 VE Day · Jun 6 D-Day · Nov 11 Veterans/Remembrance Day · Black Friday (a real discount, no timers) · Dec: gift a lifetime.
- **Launch target: early March 2027.** Be ready for Black Friday and January 2028.

### 4.3 B2B and education: a distraction for v1
- Teachers, Apple School Manager and homeschool curricula need standards alignment, kids-category rules (no third-party analytics, parental gates), a different tone and long sales cycles. Kahoot and Quizlet own classrooms.
- **Do instead:** free printable trivia lists and quizzes on the website. Teachers use them and they rank (see the ASO file).
- Revisit a homeschool family plan after 12 months if 10%+ of reviews mention kids.

### 4.5 Content risks and age rating
- **Accuracy** (Paladin's #4 complaint): 2 sources per claim (Wikipedia never the second) · a myth-check and a sources card on every story · "Report an error" in the app and a public corrections log on the website · "historians argue…" for disputed points.
- **Sensitive topics** (genocide, slavery, war crimes, religion):
  - **"Serious mode":** no costume card, no jokes, the duck only at the end with a plain line, wording that follows the scholarly consensus.
  - Never make victims the punchline. Aim humour at the powerful and the absurd.
  - No religious truth claims. No present-day party politics (cut-off ~2000).
  - Publish a one-page editorial policy.
- **Age rating:** Apple's tiers are now 4+/9+/13+/16+/18+, with the new questionnaire required since Jan 31, 2026. Aim for **13+** (infrequent mild realistic violence, no gore images). Paladin shows 12+ (the old scale).

### 4.6 Copyright
- **Public-domain art:** **The Met Open Access** (~406k CC0 images) · **Rijksmuseum** (CC0 "wherever possible") · **Library of Congress** (check each item's rights line) · **Wikimedia Commons** (check each file; many are CC BY-SA and need credit) · Natural Earth for maps.
- Photos after 1930 may still be in copyright. **Never copy Wikipedia's text** (CC BY-SA).
- **AI art:**
  - The US Copyright Office (Jan 2025) says purely AI-made images can't be copyrighted.
  - *Thaler v. Perlmutter* upheld human authorship, and the Supreme Court declined review on Mar 2, 2026.
  - So an AI-drawn duck is a mascot anyone can copy, and reviewers punish the AI look.
  - **Commission a human-drawn duck with a written copyright assignment.**

## 5. Distribution and moats
- **Formats that work for history on TikTok, Shorts and Reels:** "X explained like you're 5", drawn (Paladin's best ad) · "History's dumbest moment, day N" (a series) · myth-busts · **comment bait** ("guess the year", "put these 4 in order", answered in the next video) · "The true story Hollywood won't tell"
- **Cadence:** 1 a day, the same video on all three platforms, 45–75 s, made from the lesson's own drawings and audio.
- **Mascot narrators work:** Duolingo's ~16–17M TikTok following is built on Duo, cartoon channels lead history on YouTube, and Paladin's stick-figure ELI5 ads are its best paid creative.
- **Moats, strongest first:** (1) the voice + duck brand (compounds on social; AI clones can't own it) · (2) a checked story library with sources (trust, which Paladin lacks) · (3) SEO pages and the daily web game (compounding traffic, like JetPunk and onthisday.com) · (4) the card set + streak history (a switching cost) · (5) community (weakest; later).
- **The real threat:** Brainstash copying optional quizzes and a listen mode within months. Speed and brand are the defence.

## 6. Name
- **What great names do:** Duolingo = the mascot's name + the subject · Kahoot = a coined sound · Calm, Opal, Finch, Brilliant = real words (hard to trademark in crowded classes) · Headway, Nibble = a real word with a metaphor · Lumosity = a blend. For a solo maker, the safest strong choice is a **coined blend of two plain words**.
- **Domains are effectively gone:** of ~150 names checked through RDAP, **145 .com were registered**, even mindquill.com and dabbly.com. Only odd ones were free (histoduck, kuriobit, mindwaddle).
  - Leaders use prefixed domains (learnpaladin.com, nibble-app.com).
  - So aim for **name.app + get<name>.com + one handle everywhere**.
- **~40 raw ideas:**
  - **Coined, .app free:** Oddbit · Storybit · Lorebit · Wonderbit · Erabit · Factling · Histly · Yorely · Epocly · Tellwise · Mindpip · Mindquill · Relico · Annaly · Erawise · Pastlo · Tidbo · Yorbo · Hindsy · Lorabee · Wondary · Curiosa.

**#1 Oddbit.** Store name "Oddbit: Learn History", subtitle "History Quiz & Daily Stories".

## 7. Verdict and plan
- **Overall: 7/10, GO behind the 30-shorts gate** (up from 6.5, thanks to the format fix, the listen-mode gap and open Spanish and German markets).
- **Scores:** proof of money 7 · leader's weakness 9 · format gap (optional quizzes + listen + daily game) 8 · TikTok fit 7 · search 7 · content cost 8 · legal 8 · ceiling without ads 4 · clone threat 4.

**v1 (iOS, ~8 weeks of build after Ditch v1 is in review):**
- **Content:** 60 stories in seasons of 10, each with a 3-question recall, a card and a sources card. 90 guess-the-year puzzles. 60 duck costume cards.
- **Features:** story player (cards + audio) · Listen mode (lock-screen playlist, Pro) · streak with a weekly freeze · path/season map · hard paywall after story 1, plus a trial reminder 2 days before the charge · one review prompt after a 3-day streak · local notifications · report an error.
- **Later:** v1.1 Go deeper layer, widget, Android (Paladin earns $35k/mo there) · month 3: web daily game, 10 SEO pages, weekly-plan A/B test · month 4+: Stripe web funnel · months 6–8: Spanish, then German · then Money Stories and Philosophy courses, and Season leaderboards if retention calls for them.

**Pricing (final):**
- **Yearly $39.99 with a 7-day trial**, pre-selected. That's under the education median ($44.99) and Paladin ($59.99+).
- **Monthly $9.99**, no trial.
- **Lifetime $79.99** as a third option, never with a fake "was" price.
- **No weekly plan at launch.** Honest billing is our wedge against Paladin's ~24 billing complaints. A/B test weekly $4.99 from month 3, once there are 1k+ paywall views a week.
- **Free:** today's story as text, the daily game and the streak.
- **Web funnel at month 4+:** ~15 steps, real coupons only, the App Store as the fallback.

**First 2 weeks (no app code yet):**
- **Days 1–2:** lock the name. Book a 1-hour trademark search with a lawyer (~$150–400). Register oddbit.app and getoddbit.com, and claim @oddbitapp on TikTok, IG and YouTube.
- **Days 1–3:** Claude drafts 10 scripts from the first-30 list and the owner checks each against 2 sources. Voice test: the owner reading vs ElevenLabs on the same 2 stories.
- **Days 3–5:** build a CapCut template (cards + duck + captions). Brief 3 illustrators for the duck pose kit and collect quotes.
- **Days 4–14:** post 1 short a day to all three platforms (10–11 by day 14) and log views, shares and saves in a sheet.
- **Days 8–12:** mockups of 5 screens for approval: story player, optional recall, card drop, path map, paywall.
- **Day 14:** first gate read. **By day 30:** build if one short passes 50k views or the median passes 3k. Rethink if every short stays under 1k.

**Year 1 (gross, App Store + web, before Apple's 15%):**
| Case | Year-1 total | How |
|---|---|---|
| Bear | ~$2–4k | Shorts stay under 1k views; ~1k downloads/mo × $0.3 |
| Base | **~$20–35k** (month-12 run rate $3–5k/mo) | 3–6k downloads/mo from TikTok + a "history quiz" ranking × $0.8–1.0, plus a January bump |
| Bull | ~$120–200k | One series goes viral (20k+ downloads/mo × $1.2) + web funnel + Spanish |

**Top 5 risks:**
1. **Shorts flop.** The 30-day gate costs $0. Test 3 hook families (ELI5, dumbest moment, guess-the-year). If nothing passes, try 2 paid creator partnerships (~$300) before stopping.
2. **Brainstash copies the format.** Ship fast, own the duck voice, and build the SEO pages and daily game (slow to copy). Don't fight on ad spend.
3. **A factual or sensitive-topic blow-up.** The 2-source rule, serious mode, sources cards and a public corrections log. A history grad reviews each season (~$15–25 a story).
4. **The owner runs out of time** (med school + Ditch + 45 minutes a story). Write in seasons. Have 60 stories ready before launch, then 3–5 a week, not 7. Pay for reviewing once revenue passes $1k/mo.
5. **Low revenue per download** (tail apps earn $0.3–0.7). A hard paywall after story 1, the trial reminder (fewer refunds), the lifetime option, a January push, a web funnel from month 4. *Plus:* if the name gets blocked, the lawyer check comes before art and handles, and Lorebit is the fallback.

## Caveats
- AppKittie revenue misses web sales and can be ~10× off for small apps. Country shares are lifetime estimates across 26 countries.
- Non-English keyword scores are inflated because history = story in German, Spanish and French.
- Retention benchmarks conflict across vendors, and the Duolingo streak figures are correlations.
- TimeGuessr, Kahoot and Goalhanger figures come from third-party or press sources.
- RDAP: a 404 means likely available.
- Trademark notes come from web searches, not a USPTO clearance. Instagram wasn't checked.
- The year-1 ranges are judgment, not forecasts.
