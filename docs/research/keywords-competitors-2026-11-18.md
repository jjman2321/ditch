**What this means:**
- The App Store title should carry "Subscription Tracker".
- The subtitle should cover "cancel", "find" and "reminder", e.g. "Find, cancel & get reminders".
- The keyword field: manager, manage, track, bills, bill, reminder, trial, recurring, stop, renewal, budget, money.
- A new app with a few hundred ratings can realistically reach #3–5 for the big terms (Rocket Money and Subpilot hold #1–2), and #1 for "subscription reminder".
