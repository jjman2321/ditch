# Open search: new apps that already earn a lot (the "Tipple pattern"), any category (Nov 18, 2026)

Builds on `pivot-niches-2026-11-18.md`, `niches-round2-2026-11-18.md` and `quit-pet-2026-11-18.md` (not repeated here).
Method: AppKittie list of apps **released since Jan 1, 2025**, sorted by revenue, in 7 categories plus 18 niche searches. Then iTunes Lookup, App Store review RSS (1–3★, most recent 100), one DataForSEO call (Google US) and web search.
**All AppKittie revenue and download figures are model estimates** of App Store gross per month. Treat them as ranks, not bank statements; small apps and non-US apps are noisy.
Budget: AppKittie 218 credits (log 395 → 613, cap 615). DataForSEO $0.015.

## 1. Short answer
- **The strongest fresh niche is GLP-1 (weight-loss shot) companions.** Six apps launched since 2025 earn money. MeAgain makes ~$581k/month (est.) at ~$8.40 per download, the highest ratio in this whole search. Shotsy (2024) makes ~$366k and grew with **no paid ads** (Reddit plus word of mouth). 11% of US adults now take a GLP-1 (Gallup, Jul 2026, up from 3% in 2024). A **pet version already exists** (DreamMe, a fish, ~$16k), so the idea is proven but not taken.
- **Runner-up: crochet and knitting companions.** YarnPal makes ~$586k and Loopsy ~$141k. People search "crochet" 246k times a month on Google, and "crochet app" is up 83%. A row counter plus your projects and yarn is cheap to build, works fully on the phone, and suits a cozy duck. Loopsy's 1★ reviews complain about trial charges with no reminder, which is exactly what Ditch is known for.
- **The biggest cluster of new earners is class-action settlement finders.** Nine have launched since Jan 2025, including Settlemate (~$250k), Collect (~$100k) and ClaimHunt (~$60k). But the reviews are toxic (1.2–2.3★ average lately), the data is free on Google, and the money comes from ads promising "you're owed $2,780". An honest version would earn little and needs updating every day. **5/10. A trap.**
- **Lots of clones, no money:** walking pets (6 launched, all ~$0), water-reminder pets, skincare, pelvic floor, hormone-cycle and social-anxiety apps. None of the 2025–26 launches earns more than ~$4k.
- **High money that doesn't fit us:** camera heart-rate and blood-pressure apps (~$376k–$700k, health-claim risk), AI short dramas and AI companions (licensed content plus AI costs), AI manifestation audio (Stella ~$315k, AI voice costs), and Bible widgets (Monkey Taps ~$701k; no fit for the duck).
- **Updated top 5:** (1) duck-pond quit app **8.0**, (2) GLP-1 duck companion **7.5**, (3) crochet/knit duck companion **7.0**, (4) screen-time blocker 6.5, (5) wake-up alarm 6.0.
- **GLP-1 is the only idea that challenges the quit app on money.** It earns more per user and its growth is proven without ads. It loses on brand fit: the "Ditch" name doesn't fit, users skew older than 23–40, and it carries more health and medication risk. Next step: test both cheaply (see §6).

## 4. Deep dives
### 4a. GLP-1 shot-day duck companion (7.5)
- **Leaders:** MeAgain ~$581k (AI companion, food scanner; collects "Sensitive Info" linked to you, 18+); Shotsy ~$366k (no ads, ASO plus Reddit); Glowise (6.7k ratings), Pep ~$32k, DreamMe pet ~$16k, Glapp ~$10k, plus 10+ small ones.
- **Review gaps (recent 1–3★):**
  - Free features moved behind a paywall ("terrible rope in", Glapp; "$40 a year to see things").
  - Updates that remove paid features, plus "an AI thing no one asked for" (Pep: 67 of its last 100 reviews ≤3★; its dose calculator was removed).
  - Charged after deleting the app before the trial ended ("$70", DreamMe).
  - A free tier so thin you can't log water (DreamMe).
  - The pill isn't supported ("not set up for the pill").
  - People want a "maintenance mode" for after the weight loss.
  - Food logging is clunky or double-logs from Apple Health (MeAgain).
- **How they grow:** organic search and Reddit GLP-1 communities (Shotsy's founder: "hasn't spent a dollar on ads"). Big Google demand around side effects, and creators post their GLP-1 journeys on TikTok.
- **Our angle:** "Your shot-day buddy."
  - Weekly shot or daily pill reminders: our reminder engine, already built and tested.
  - Injection-site rotation, a side-effect and "food noise" log, quick-add buttons for protein and water, weight, and a doctor-visit PDF.
  - The duck's pond grows each week you stay on plan. A missed dose never punishes you. The dry, kind voice suits a topic full of shame.
  - Pill mode and maintenance/taper mode from day one.
  - **Data Not Collected:** a strong selling point for sensitive health data.
  - A written promise that **free features never move behind the paywall**.
  - No food database, no AI, no dose calculator.
- **Moat:** months of dose, weight and side-effect history (people don't switch mid-treatment), the pond they've grown, trust and privacy, and the doctor report.
- **Pricing:** competitors charge $39.99–59.99/yr and $9.99–14.99/mo (Shotsy); MeAgain goes up to $119.99.
  - Ours: free core tracking and reminders forever.
  - Pro (charts, doctor PDF, full pond, widget, iCloud): **$34.99/yr (7-day trial with a reminder before it ends) · $5.99/mo · $59.99 lifetime**.
- **Risks:**
  - Apple 1.4.1 (medical): no dosing advice and no claims.
  - Users skew 35–64 (Gallup: highest use at 50–64), outside the 23–40 core.
  - The top is crowded (two apps with 35–41k ratings).
  - Drug names are trademarks: use them as text with ®, like competitors, and no drug logos.
  - "Ditch" doesn't fit, and "ditch the weight" reads as body shaming. It needs a duck-led name. No duck GLP-1 apps found on the App Store; trademark search not done.
- **Year 1 (net after Apple's 15%, at month 12):**
  - Bear (40%): ~500 downloads/mo × $0.60 ≈ **$250/mo**.
  - Base (45%): ~2,500 × $1.50 ≈ **$3.2k/mo**.
  - Bull (15%): ~10,000 × $3.00 ≈ **$25k/mo** (a Reddit/ASO hit like Shotsy).
  - Newcomers earn $0.60–1.50 per download (DreamMe, Glapp, Pep). The $8–12 per download belongs to the leaders.

### 4b. Crochet/knit duck companion (7.0)
- **Leaders:** YarnPal ~$586k (courses, videos, 500+ patterns, AI stitch ID; $39.99/yr); Loopsy ~$141k (pattern and stash manager, counters, PDF/website import; $4.99–99.99); LoopCraft ~$16k; older My Row Counter (4.0k ratings), knitCompanion (6.9k), kntd (Ravelry helper). Ravelry (web) is free.
- **Review gaps:**
  - Loopsy (52 of 100 recent ≤3★):
    - It forces a trial with no free version to try first.
    - **No reminder before the $43 charge, no refund.**
    - Advertised as free, but capped at 3 yarns and 6 patterns.
    - Charged $30 straight from an Instagram link.
    - Rating pop-ups appear before you've even used it.
  - LoopCraft: few patterns, no stash, no videos, and "a derivative work from a designer" (copyright anger).
- **How they grow:** Instagram and TikTok crochet creators (Loopsy reviews mention Instagram links). The Gen Z crochet boom fits 20–40 women well. Data on their ad spend is thin.
- **Our angle:** "A cozy craft buddy that never surprises your card."
  - Unlimited row and stitch counters, free forever.
  - Projects with photos, yarn stash, and your own PDF patterns with a line highlighter (we never host or rewrite other people's patterns).
  - Every finished project adds something to the duck's nest (scarf, hat, blanket). A "finished objects" shelf.
  - Trial-end reminder, Data Not Collected, works offline.
  - Later: lock-screen counter (Live Activity) and an Apple Watch tap counter.
- **Moat:** stash, project history, photos and pattern library live in the app; a cozy brand crafters love; creator partnerships.
- **Pricing:** free counters and projects. Pro (unlimited patterns and stash, highlighter, widget, iCloud): **$24.99/yr · $3.99/mo · $39.99 lifetime** (crafters like one-time purchases).
- **Risks:**
  - The name "Ditch" means the opposite of making things, so it needs a new name.
  - PDF markup needs a native module (dev build).
  - We can't match YarnPal's video courses cheaply.
  - The pattern-copyright line must stay strict.
- **Year 1 (net):**
  - Bear (40%) ~600 × $0.40 ≈ **$200/mo**.
  - Base (45%) ~3,000 × $1.00 ≈ **$2.6k/mo**.
  - Bull (15%) ~12,000 × $2.00 ≈ **$20k/mo**.

### 4c. Class-action settlement finder (5.0: the most new earners, but a trap)
- **Leaders:** Settlemate ~$250k, Collect ~$100k, ClaimHunt ~$60k, Payout ~$55k, GetPaid ~$46k, MoneyPilot ~$23k, PayMe ~$10k. Paywalls run $4.99–5.99/wk and $29.99–59.99/yr.
- **Review gaps:**
  - "Pay to get paid", and "it's free on Google".
  - "Says I missed out on $2,780", then a paywall (ClaimHunt's TikTok-style ads).
  - Closed or wrong listings.
  - "Filed every claim for a year, never paid."
  - Retyping your details for every claim.
  - Charges after the trial.
  - MoneyPilot: 85 of its last 90 reviews ≤2★.
- **How they grow:** paid social with inflated "you're owed $X" hooks.
- **Our honest angle:**
  - A free list with real typical payouts ($5–50).
  - Reminders before claim deadlines.
  - Your details saved on the phone with copy buttons.
  - A "checks on the way" jar.
- **Why only 5.0:**
  - No moat (the data is public).
  - The honest version gives up the hook that drives the revenue.
  - Someone has to curate the list daily, forever.
  - FTC and App Review risk.
- **Better use:** a free "open settlements this week" page on ditchsubs.app for search traffic ("open class action settlements" +25%), feeding the subscription tracker.
- **Year 1 (net):** bear ~$130, base ~$1.7k, bull ~$13k/mo.

### 4d. Quick notes on #3–4
- **Couples:** Candle's update added a character and gamification. 53 of its last 100 reviews are ≤3★ ("makes it seem more like a game than meaningful"). That's a warning that game layers can hurt intimate apps. It also needs two-phone sync (a server or CloudKit sharing), and "Waddle: Couples Questions" already exists.
- **ADHD body double:** Cat On Chair (~$21k) proves "a pet sits with you while you work", but the money is low. Better as a feature inside the quit or focus idea.

## 6. What I'd do
1. Keep the quit pond as plan A. Run a 2-week test of both ideas: ~5 duck TikToks each ("Shot day. The duck remembered." vs "Your vape is a $1,300 subscription") plus two waitlist pages. Build whichever pulls more sign-ups per view.
2. All three top ideas share one engine: duck companion + local reminders + honest paywall with a trial reminder + Data Not Collected. Build that engine once.
3. Before naming anything, get a trademark check (USPTO search not done here).
