# Which niches fit the Ditch brand and earn more than a subscription tracker? (Nov 18, 2026)

AppKittie revenue/downloads are model estimates (ranks, not accounts). Budget: AppKittie 187 credits (~1,880 → ~1,693), DataForSEO $0.035. Bar to beat: subscription tracker demand 5/10, base ~$1.9k/month net at month 12 (`demand-2026-11-18.md`).

## Short answer
- **Best fit: a "Ditch it" quit app** (vaping/nicotine, drinking, sugar, weed, doomscrolling), 7.5/10. Base ~$4.4k/month net at month 12 (~2.3× the tracker). Every brand piece does a job: the name is the verb, DITCHED = quit day, the money number ("your vape costs $1,300 a year"), the duck as a daily companion.
- **Screen time / app blockers earn the most** (BePresent ~$920k, Opal ~$737k/month) but need Apple's FamilyControls permission and native Swift extensions. 6.5.
- **Photo cleaners are top earners** (Swipewipe ~$1M/month) but saturated, paid-ads and weekly-plan driven. 6.0.
- **Name blocker:** Ditch Labs Inc. holds a registered DITCH trademark (Reg. 7296301, class 42) for a quit-vaping app + DitchPen; "Ditch: Quit Zyn & Pouches" launched Jul 2026; "Ditch: Pay Off Debt Faster" owns debt payoff (~$200k/month est.); DitchIt LLC holds DITCHIT (marketplace). **Lawyer check before any quit-app build.**
- **Recommendation: two apps under the brand.** Ship the subscription tracker; build "Ditch it" (working name) from the same codebase after the lawyer check. Not a mode inside the tracker.

## #1 "Ditch it": quit a costly habit
- Competitors: I Am Sober $242k/mo (191k ratings), Smoke $62k, Days Since $49k (multi-habit), Smoke Free $36k, Kwit $35k, Sober Time $25k, Quit Vaping $18.7k, Grounded (weed) $12.8k, Puff Count (founder reported $40k MRR at peak; $9.99/week).
- Review gaps: "why pay to count days?", trial bait-and-switch, accounts and personal data required, paid widgets, ads, fake community profiles.
- Positioning: "Your vape is a $1,300-a-year subscription. Ditch it." Free counter forever, no account, quit stays on your phone; honest paywall and "Data Not Collected" become the selling point.
- Moat: per-habit money math; a slip never wipes your total; duck as daily companion (check-ins, milestones, widget); several habits with one custom product page each (keyword-assigned CPPs); website cost calculators ("What does your vape cost per year?").
- Pricing: free unlimited counters and totals; Pro = more than 2 habits, widget, craving tools, custom check-ins, iCloud backup; $29.99/yr · $4.99/mo · $49.99 lifetime.
- Growth: TikTok proven (Puff Count, QUITTR); Reddit large but strict.
- Risks: trademark; Apple 1.4.1 (no medical claims; cite NHS/CDC for milestones); alcohol-withdrawal safety line and crisis numbers; teen vapers (age rating, never target minors); tone; DECISIONS #13 "no streaks" needs its own decision for this app.
- Year 1: bear ~$400/mo at month 12 (35%), base ~$4.4k (45%), bull ~$19k (20%); >$1k/month by month 12 ~55% (tracker ~35%).
- Reuse ~60–70%: money/dates/reminders domain, notifications, StoreKit, SQLite, Sunflower UI, hold-to-ditch, total reveal, paywall, duck, voice, website tools/SEO. New: habit model, day counter, craving SOS, sourced milestones, journal, widget (~4–6 weeks).

## #2 Screen time blocker (6.5): BePresent $920k, Opal $737k, one sec $55k (indie). Strong but hardest build (FamilyControls approval, Swift extensions). Base ~$5.1k/month at month 12, ~2–3 months later launch.
## #3 Photo cleaner (6.0): Swipewipe $1.04M; saturated, paid-ads, spam-guideline risk. Base ~$2.4k/month.

## Recommendation
1. Book the 1-hour trademark check now (Ditch Labs DITCH, Ditch: Quit Zyn, DitchIt) and ask whether "Ditch it" or a duck-led name works for a quit app.
2. Ship Ditch: Subscription Tracker (built, tested, website live); re-check after ~1,000 downloads.
3. Test demand cheaply: ~10 short duck videos on TikTok/Instagram ("your vape is a $1,300/yr subscription") + a waitlist page.
4. Build app #2 from the shared codebase (~4–6 weeks), leading with vaping/nicotine and drinking via per-habit product pages.
Neither app likely reaches $10k/month in year 1 (quit app ~15–20%, tracker ~5%).
