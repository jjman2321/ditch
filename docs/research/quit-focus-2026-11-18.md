## Findings
- **Focused beats generic.** Multi-addiction trackers earn little; the money sits in apps that name one habit in the title (Quit Vaping, Smoke Free, Reframe, Tipple). I Am Sober supports many habits but is branded around one ("sober").
- The App Store title is the strongest ASO field, so the title must pick one habit. Subtitle + keyword field + one custom product page per habit cover the rest.
- **Alcohol = biggest money** (~8× the vaping pool, higher $/download, Tipple's pet ~$10/download) but three entrenched leaders, a pet rival, and withdrawal safety.
- **Nicotine (vape + pouches) = most winnable:** no dominant app, no real pet rival, easy keywords ("quit vaping" 83/28, "quit zyn" 66/24), young TikTok-native audience, pouches still an open land-grab. Lower $/download.

## Recommendation
One app, built multi-habit under the hood, **marketed as focused**:
- Title leads with **nicotine**: e.g. "Puddle: Quit Vaping & Zyn". Subtitle names the others ("Quit drinking, weed, scrolling").
- Day-one custom product pages: vaping, pouches, drinking, weed, scrolling.
- After ~6–8 weeks, compare conversion and $/download per page. If drinking clearly wins, swap the title to drinking.
