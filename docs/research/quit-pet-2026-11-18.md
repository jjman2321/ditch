Pattern: a pet + quitting reached ~$124k/month in ~6 months (Tipple), but on paid ads and an aggressive paywall. Copycats are arriving fast (4 launched in 2026).

## Tipple's 1–3★ reviews (Nov 2026, most recent 30): the gap
- **No reminder before the trial ends, then charged $99:** the most common complaint (~12 of 30). "No way to cancel", "no refund", "scam".
- **The game runs out:** "after a week your camp is fully upgraded", "stopped updating after several days", "not rewarded after two weeks".
- **Too many personal questions before trying; data distrust.**
- **AI chatbot disliked** ("Booooo AI!").
- **Guilt mechanics:** "unlucky" days; feeling like you "failed" or are "starting over".
- **Bugs; cut off on iPhone SE.**
Every one of these is something Ditch already does differently: trial-end reminders are our core feature, honest paywall, no account, Data Not Collected, no AI, every screen fits SE, slips never wipe progress.

## Moat (honest)
No tech moat: a counter + pet is copyable in weeks (see the clones). A small app's real moats:
1. **Attachment and stored progress:** months of your duck's pond and milestones live in the app; people don't restart a pet they've raised (Finch's retention model).
2. **Depth of the game:** content for a year (seasons, pond friends, milestones per habit), so it never "runs out after a week" like Tipple.
3. **Trust in a scammy category:** honest trial, reminder before the trial ends, no data. Shows up in reviews, ratings and word of mouth.
4. **Brand and audience:** the duck on TikTok; a character people recognize is hard to copy.

## Concept sketch: "Ditch" duck pond (working name; trademark check first)
- Pick what you're ditching (vape, drinks, weed, sugar, scrolling). Hold to ditch = quit day.
- Each day off grows the duck's pond: lily pads, friends, seasons, real milestones (NHS/CDC-sourced) as pond events.
- Money kept is shown as real things ("$43: that's a new pair of trainers").
- Slip? The duck stays with you; nothing dies, nothing resets, the pond keeps what you built. Dry, kind voice.
- Craving SOS: the duck's 2-minute distraction; optional "pause before TikTok" card.
- Free: 1 habit, counter, pond basics. Pro: more habits, full pond seasons, widget, craving tools, iCloud backup. 7-day trial with a reminder before it ends.
Score: **8/10** (was 7.5 for a plain quit counter): proven demand (Tipple), clear review gap, emotional and gamified. Risks: trademark, art workload (duck redraw + pond assets), paid ads may be needed to match Tipple's growth, Apple 1.4.1 health claims.
