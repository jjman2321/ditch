# What 5,535 competitor reviews say (Nov 11, 2026)

Every App Store review Apple's public feed returns (up to 500 newest per app per country) for 17 apps in our niche, from the US, UK, Canada and Australia. Every review was read, in four groups. Counts are keyword tallies checked by reading, so treat them as "~". Quotes are verbatim and were script-checked against the source.

AppKittie had 0 credits (it charges 1 credit per review), so it was not used. No Google Play reviews and no new revenue estimates; revenue figures below are from `autosync.md` (Nov 9).

## Top 10 findings across all 17 apps

1. **Reminders that don't fire kill trackers.** ~70 Bobby reviews (2016–2025) and ~55 Bill Tracker Pro reviews (3 years broken) say reminders never came, came late, or said the wrong thing. ~16 people missed a real payment ("Missed my mortgage payment due to this." Bill Tracker Pro 1★). Users couldn't tell a bug from a settings problem, because nothing showed what was scheduled.
2. **Data loss is the biggest single source of 1★ (~210 reviews).** Mostly after an update or a new phone ("Spent 2 hours inputting all my bills….glitched and all my bill info disappeared" My Bills 1★). Two update-day spikes (Chronicle v8, Bill Tracker Pro v3.12.20) made ~45 1★ reviews in days.
3. **A free limit revealed after setup is the #1 "scam" trigger (~155 reviews).** People are angry about *when* they found out, not about paying: "If it had told me that at the beginning I would've been prepared & may have upgraded" (Bills Organizer). Caps of 2–5 items read as "not free" and "bait".
4. **"A subscription to cancel subscriptions" is the core objection (~115 reviews).** "I don't want to spend money so that I can be reminded to spend money." One-time prices earn praise (~75 reviews); cheap yearly works when a person stands behind it (Chronicle ~$12/yr). Our $29.99/yr is at the top of what these users accept.
5. **Overpromising is the fastest route to 1★.** "We cancel for you" that turns out to be instructions (~85), auto-detect that misses or finds nothing (~92), ads that lie (~110). "If I knew all the subscriptions I had, I wouldn't need the app." (Feenko 1★)
6. **Can't cancel the canceller (~125).** Web checkouts that don't show in iPhone Subscriptions, billing names like "Finelo", apps that list every sub except their own. "They list all subs except for their own" (Rocket Money 1★).
7. **Bank linking is a cost (~95).** Failed links, banks locking accounts, spam calls, SSN requests. "This went directly for my bank log in and password. Sounds fishy to me." The flip side: "no bank login, no account" is named in ~55 good reviews.
8. **The yearly total is the "aha" (~120 love reviews).** "Bit of a shocker to see the annual cost." (Orbit 4★) · "I saved $924 a year on a subscription I didn't know I had!!!" (Subpilot 5★). 5★ reviews quote dollar figures.
9. **A human who answers flips ratings.** ~80 loves for a named developer who fixes things fast; a dozen-plus reviews say "upgraded to 5★ after the dev replied". ~195 hate silence or a bouncing support email ("this is just a little side project so "good luck"." Bobby 1★).
10. **Early or repeated rating prompts backfire.** "Keep begging for reviews before I've had a chance to use the app is a sure way to get deleted." Prompts in onboarding produce junk 5★s that are really complaints. The good 5★ reviews are written in moments of relief.

## Must avoid (seen to destroy trust)
- Any limit revealed after the user has done setup work.
- Calling anything "free" without the limit next to it.
- "We find / cancel your subscriptions" in the app, App Store page or ads.
- Web checkout, a billing name other than "Ditch", paid trials, stacked add-ons, weekly plans, % of savings.
- Moving anything a paying user has behind a newer paywall; re-charging past buyers; ads.
- Forced email or account sign-up.
- An update that can wipe or duplicate data; a support address that bounces.
- Rating prompts on open, in onboarding, or more than once in a while.
- Reminders for things the user already handled (ditched, deleted).
- Selling Pro import before it works reliably (Orbit: "the import features … simply don't work for me", 2★ refund).

## Method
- Apps found with the iTunes Search API (10 terms, US + UK) and past research; reviews from `itunes.apple.com/{country}/rss/customerreviews/…/json`, 10 pages × 4 countries per app.
- Four parallel readers, one per group, each reading every review and tallying themes with `python3 -I`; quotes script-checked word for word.
- Raw data (not committed): the session scratchpad `reviews/all.json`.
