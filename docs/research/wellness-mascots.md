# Mascot-led wellness apps: onboarding, mascot and paywall research

Research for Ditch's onboarding and paywall. Done 2026-11-09.

## How this was done
- Source: Mobbin, iOS. Every screen in the tables below was opened and read at full size (Mobbin previews skip screens, so each flow was downloaded screen by screen).
- About 300 screens read across 30 flows. Some screens repeat between flows; each is described once.
- Apps covered, all with a real character: **Finch** (bird), **Ahead** (ghost "Kai"), **Gentler Streak** (Yorhart), **Flo** (Flori), plus **Wysa** (penguin), **Me+** (chicken), **BitePal** (raccoon).
- Checked and left out because they have no character: Tiimo (abstract shapes and line art), Forest (trees, no face), Fabulous, Rise, Stoic. Habitica is not on Mobbin.
- Mobbin is static. Motion is described from what the stills imply (frame sequences, changing poses) and from web sources, cited.
- "↗" links go to the exact Mobbin screen. Flow links are in each heading.
- Prices are as captured on Mobbin. Many are in SGD (S$) because Mobbin captured them in Singapore.

## Contents
1. Screen-by-screen tables (Finch, Ahead, Gentler Streak, Flo, Wysa, Me+, BitePal)

## 1.2 Ahead: Emotions Coach

Mascot: a lilac ghost-shaped blob with two dot eyes and a small smile. Many costume variants (rocket, notebook, hourglass, bell, cheerleader pom-poms, angry red version). # 2. Patterns

## 2.1 Length and pacing

| App | Onboarding screens before home | Paywall placement | Mascot on paywall? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finch | ~30 (incl. pet setup and goals) | After the starter plan, before Day 1 | Pre-screen only ("free forever" bird). Price screens have no mascot |
| Ahead | ~36 + 10 paywall screens | After quiz, commitments, notification ask | Pre-screen ghost peeking; "reminder" screen with ghost + bell |
| Gentler Streak | 17 | **Not in onboarding.** Pop-up offer on home, then a quiet card | No |
| Flo | ~45 | After a "hold to commit" pledge | No |
| Wysa | 15 | Mid-flow, as a therapist upsell | No |
| Me+ | 30 | After a signed "contract" | No (battery people) |
| BitePal | 19+ | After onboarding; also on home | Yes, the mascot acts out each feature |
| **Ditch (now)** | **7** | After reminders ask | Yes, jumping raccoon |

- Long flows are broken up with mascot interstitials ("Halfway to your results!", quest maps, "But enough about me.").
- Every long flow has a visible sense of progress: thin bar (Ahead, Flo, Me+, Finch goals), "Question 1 of 10" (Ahead), a quest map with locked steps (Flo), weekday name (Finch "DAY 1").
- Gentler Streak has no progress bar and still feels short, because each screen is one idea with big type.
- Ditch's 7 screens are short and every one collects real data. Short is a strength. Do not pad it.

## 2.2 How the mascot builds attachment
1. **It introduces itself and talks in first person.** "Hi, I'm Yorhart", "Hey! I'm wysa", "I'm Flori, your very own Well-Being Battery!", "Cheep cheep, thanks for hatching me."
2. **The user answers back with the button.** "Hi, Yorhart", "Yes, totally!", "I'm ready", "Go for it". The button is a line of dialogue, not "Next".
3. **It learns your name and uses it.** Finch, Gentler Streak, Wysa, Flo, Ahead all ask. Wysa uses it in every title ("JS, how old are you?").
4. **It reacts to what you do.** Yorhart grins while you type. Ahead's ghost changes face with the slider. Finch's bird gets a lightbulb when you pick goals. Finch's egg recolours as you tap. BitePal's bubble changes to the tone you picked.
5. **Ownership.** You hatch it (Finch), open the bin to find it (BitePal), name it (Finch, BitePal), pick its pronouns and trait (Finch), pick how it talks (BitePal).
6. **It has states that mirror yours.** Flori is a battery whose charge shows how you feel. Yorhart lies down when you overdo it and sits in a deck chair when you're on a break. Ahead shows a red angry ghost vs a calm one.
7. **Costumes per topic.** Flori wears a sleep mask for sleep questions and binoculars on the quest map. Ahead's ghost holds an hourglass for procrastination, a bell for reminders. The mascot is the icon system.
8. **Never punished.** Finch shows missed days as "repaired", not broken. Gentler Streak gives "full permission to rest". Reviews say Finch's bird "doesn't suffer" when you skip ([Headway review](https://makeheadway.com/blog/finch-app-review/)).

## 2.3 Mascot size and placement
- **Talk screens** (intro, interstitial, celebration): mascot is the hero, 30 to 45% of the screen, centred, text above or in a bubble.
- **Work screens** (questions, lists, forms): mascot shrinks to 60 to 80pt at the top, or peeks over the card edge (Me+, Wysa, BitePal), or disappears (Ahead's plan screens, Flo's calendar).
- **Price screens**: most apps remove the mascot entirely (Flo, Gentler Streak, Ahead's timeline and plan list, Finch's trial terms). Where it stays (BitePal) it acts out features.
- **Loaders**: mascot becomes the spinner (Ahead's eyes, Finch's bird in a ring, Flo's grey battery).

## 2.4 Personalisation
- Ask for a name early, then use it (Finch, Wysa, Gentler Streak, Flo, Ahead).
- Reflect answers back in the next line: "Got it! ..." (Me+), expanding answer rows with "here's how we help" (Flo), "Become a better manager with Ahead Pro!" from the career goal (Ahead).
- First personal result before the paywall: Ahead's scores, Flo's "you're likely in your luteal phase", Finch's starter plan. Ditch already has the best version of this: the real yearly total.
- Smart defaults the user can fix: Finch suggests a pet name + "Shuffle", BitePal pre-fills "Nomad" + dice, Flo pre-fills "5 day period / Adjust for smarter predictions".

## 2.5 Permission asks
| App | Where | Pre-screen wording | Promise or proof | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Finch | Early, right after bonding | "Get reminders from Lee" + mock "From Lee · Remember to drink water!" | Mock signed by the pet | "Maybe later" |
| Ahead | After commitment, before paywall (or after paywall in a variant) | "Want to remind your future self to keep going?" | "*We'll only send you 1 notification per day"; "88% more likely" | "Not now" |
| Gentler Streak | After Health + profile | "Keep your momentum." + home-screen mock | – | "Skip" (same size as "Go for it") |
| Wysa | After paywall | "Before you start" | – | "NOT NOW" |
| BitePal | Mid-flow, then choose timing | "We'll support you to keep logging" + notification mock | "2x faster" | "Set up later" |
| Gentler Streak (Health) | Mid-flow | "Fill me in, Alex!" | "Your health data will never leave this device." | – |

- All use a custom pre-screen before the iOS prompt.
- The best ones show a realistic notification mock and say how often you'll hear from them.
- Finch asks again later, in context, inside a sheet ("Lee will remind you when it's time to complete your goal!").

## 2.6 Paywall timing and wording
- Two camps: **paywall at the end of onboarding** (Finch, Ahead, Flo, Me+, BitePal) and **paywall later, from the product** (Gentler Streak's home card; Finch's settings banner and earned preview).
- Pre-paywall "soft" screens soften the ask: "Finch is free forever / But we'd love for you to try Finch Plus for 7 days free too!" and Ahead's "We'll send you a reminder before your free trial ends".
- Price shown next to the button is the clearest: Gentler Streak "1 week free, then S$ 64.98/year" directly above "Start Free Trial".
- Mission and honesty lines after purchase: Finch founders' thank-you, Gentler Streak "your support is our sole source of income".

## 2.7 Trial wording
- The 3-step timeline is now standard: Finch, Ahead (twice), Flo (after purchase). Steps: Today → reminder day (Day 5 or "2 days before") → charge day.
- Finch writes the real charge date ("You will be charged on Aug 30"). Ahead uses relative days only ("Day 7").
- Ahead and Flo send the trial reminder **by email**, which needs an account. Finch sends a **push notification**.
- Apple has rejected trial **toggles** since mid-January 2026 under Guideline 3.1.2; the fix is a clear plan option that says whether it includes a trial ([RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/r-i-p-toggle-paywall-we-hardly-knew-ye/), [Superwall](https://superwall.com/blog/external-checkout-a-b-testing-and-trial-toggles-confirmed-apples-rules-for-ios)). Mobbin shows Flo's change: old toggle ("Not sure yet? Enable free trial.") → new radio option ("Not sure yet? Start a free trial").
- Blinkist's trial-timeline paywall is reported to have cut complaints by 55% and lifted conversion by 23% ([UX Planet](https://uxplanet.org/how-solving-our-biggest-customer-complaint-at-blinkist-led-to-a-23-increase-in-conversion-b60ad514134b)); secondary sources, not Blinkist data.

## 2.8 Emotional tone
- **Finch:** soft, childlike, puns ("Happy Satur-Yay!"), "cheep". Celebrates tiny wins ("2 days of Calm goals").
- **Gentler Streak:** adult, kind, permission-giving ("You have full permission to rest and relax."). Humour in small print ("We're as allergic to it as you are.").
- **Ahead:** playful and cheeky ("hot like a jalapeño", "cat videos"), then hard-sell.
- **Flo:** warm and validating ("We get it —"), clinical proof, then aggressive upsells.
- **Wysa:** quiet, private, minimal.
- **Me+:** hype, fear-based ("Say goodbye to distress", "Anxiety and depression").
- **BitePal:** cute and cheeky; user can choose the mascot's tone.

## 3.3 Motion rules worth copying (all honest)
1. One idle loop on talk screens: slow breathe (scale 1.00 → 1.02) and a blink every 3 to 5 s.
2. React to input: a pose or face change on tap, type or value change. Max one reaction per action.
3. Count up real numbers (Ditch's total) instead of a fake "analysing" loader.
4. Celebrate in place (Finch's confetti on the list) instead of a new screen when the action is small.
5. Reduce Motion: swap every move for a cross-fade and a static pose.

# 4. What works and what doesn't

## 4.1 What works (and fits Ditch's rules)
- **Mascot talks first, user answers with the button** (Gentler Streak "Hi, Yorhart"; Flo "I'm ready").
- **Mascot reacts to input** (Gentler Streak, Ahead, Finch). Cheap, delightful, honest.
- **Big mascot on talk screens, small on work screens, gone on price details.**
- **Realistic notification mock + a frequency promise** (Finch mock; Ahead "1 notification per day").
- **Equal-weight "Skip"** next to the yes button (Gentler Streak).
- **Smart default + edit** (Flo's 5-day default; Finch's suggested name with Shuffle).
- **Honest "I don't know" exits** (Flo "I don't remember").
- **Label mock data as sample data** (Flo "NOTE: This is sample data").
- **Empty state that says when value arrives** (Gentler Streak "ready 7 days after your next recorded workout").
- **Trial timeline with a real date** (Finch "charged on Aug 30").
- **Earned free preview with no card** (Finch "This preview is on us! You won't be charged.").
- **Free-forever framing before the offer** (Finch "Finch is free forever").
- **Honest money line after purchase** (Gentler Streak "your support is our sole source of income"; Finch founders' thank-you).
- **Privacy line under the permission button** (Gentler Streak "Your health data will never leave this device"; Wysa "no login").
- **Mascot mirrors state without guilt** (Yorhart's deck chair; Finch's repaired streak).

## 4.2 What doesn't (flags against Ditch's honesty rules)
| Tactic | Where | Breaks Ditch rule |

# 5. Apply to Ditch, screen by screen

Context: Ditch v2 = teal raccoon, small avatar + speech bubble on work screens, hero raccoon on Welcome, Reveal and Paywall, Nunito, fat 3D buttons, one thick progress bar. Flow: Welcome → Tick → Reveal → Dates → Reminders → Paywall → Pick 3 → Home.

## 5.0 Global changes
1. **Give the raccoon a name and let it introduce itself once** (Yorhart, Wysa, Flori pattern). Owner picks the name. Not "Rocket". Keep it short and not a real brand.
2. **Buttons that answer the raccoon** on talk screens only. Keep plain verbs on work screens.
3. **Two sizes only.** Hero (~40% height) on Welcome, Reveal, Paywall top, You're set. Avatar (56 to 64pt, left of bubble) on Tick, Dates, Reminders, Pick 3. Never on top of a button (CLAUDE.md rule 8).
4. **Reaction, not decoration.** On each work screen the avatar changes pose once in response to the user (tick count rising, date picked, permission granted).
5. **Idle:** blink every 3 to 5 s and a slow breathe on hero screens. Reduce Motion: static + cross-fade.
6. **Haptics:** `selectionAsync` on ticks and day taps; `notificationAsync(Success)` on permission granted, trial started, "Ditched!". Nothing on screen load. Nothing on the paywall appearing.
7. **Copy voice:** first person from the raccoon in bubbles ("I'll..."), plain second person in headlines. No "!" on more than one line per screen.
8. **Stay clear of BitePal.** BitePal's raccoon lives in a trash can and pops out of it ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/167bab5d-99d8-4407-96a7-d065e5586685), [screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/1e19816e-88c6-419e-9258-b336cfe58722)), has thick black outlines, a grey body, a heart on the chest, and lets you name it and pick its tone. ## 5.6 Paywall
**Now:** X, RESTORE, jumping celebrating raccoon, "Get a heads-up before all 6 renewals.", 6 logos tagged FREE/FREE/FREE/PRO/PRO/PRO, 4 features, Yearly "SAVE 50%" $29.99/yr, "START 7-DAY FREE TRIAL", "Then $29.99 a year from Mon, Nov 16. No need to cancel today, I'll remind you Sat, Nov 14.", "CONTINUE FREE: 3 REMINDERS".

What's already better than every app studied: close from frame one, real dates, a real free button, the user's own subs split into FREE and PRO. Keep all of that.

**Change:**
- **Raccoon pose:** jumping celebration reads as "paying = party". Use a calm pose holding the bell, ~100 to 120pt, beside or above the headline (Ahead's ghost-with-bell screen is the one place a mascot helps a paywall). No confetti.
- **Sticky bottom block** (Gentler Streak): price line → main button → "Continue free" → Restore + Terms + Privacy. Plans and timeline scroll above.
- **Timeline visible, not below the fold:** a compact 3-step row: "Today · Pro starts free" → "Sat, Nov 14 · I remind you" → "Mon, Nov 16 · $29.99 charged". Real dates, from today.
- **Reminder promise depends on permission.** If notifications are off, the line "I'll remind you Sat, Nov 14" is false. Show instead: "Turn on notifications so I can remind you Sat, Nov 14." with a button that opens the permission or Settings.
- **Dogfood the trial.** On trial start, add "Ditch Pro (trial)" to the user's own list with a reminder 2 days before the charge. Show it on the next screen (5.8). It's the product proving itself. Owner to decide if it counts toward the 3 free reminders (suggest: no, it's a system reminder).
- **"SAVE 50%"** is true ($29.99 vs 12 × $4.99 = $59.88 → 49.9%). Keep the math visible ("$2.50/mo, billed yearly") and avoid a strikethrough, which reads like the fake anchors in section 4.2.
- **Lifetime:** keep the existing honest line about bank sync being separate (CLAUDE.md rule 7).
- No toggle. Trial lives only on the yearly plan, labelled in the plan row (Apple Jan 2026).

**Pick:** "Get a heads-up before all 6 renewals." / sub "Free covers Netflix, Amazon Prime and YouTube. Pro covers the rest." / "Start 7-day free trial" / "Continue free: 3 reminders" / under-button line as is, conditional on permission.

## 5.7 Pick your 3 free reminders
**Now:** worried raccoon holding cash + "Free gets 3 reminders. I picked your biggest. Swap any time!", list with 3 pre-ticked, "3 OF 3 PICKED", "DONE".

**Change:**
- Pre-ticked biggest three is a good smart default (Finch starter plan, Flo 5-day default). Keep.
- Pose: "worried with cash" fits the money theme, but "worried" on a free-path screen can read as guilt (Finch's sad grey bird). Use a neutral "pointing at the list" pose.
- When the user taps a 4th: shake the counter gently and say "Free covers 3. Untick one to swap." Never pop the paywall again here.
