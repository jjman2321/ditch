# SEO research: keywords, competitors, where we stand (Nov 15, 2026)

Source: DataForSEO (Google US, desktop), $0.18 spent. Search volumes are DataForSEO's monthly estimates; KD = keyword difficulty 0–100 (lower is easier). Raw files in the session scratchpad (`dfs/`).

## Where Ditch stands
- ditchsubs.app has **no rankings yet** in DataForSEO's data. Normal for a site this new: Google hasn't indexed it properly. Search Console + Bing (owner tasks) are the first step.
- Not in the top 10 for any of the 21 searches checked.

Takeaway: the other subscription apps are small. Who wins the big searches:
- **Price searches:** the official sites, NerdWallet and tech media.
- **Cancel searches:** the official help pages, plus thin "converter" sites (flixicam, muconvert).
- **AI comparisons:** small blogs (techlusive, tuningdigital, aionx, vantagepoint, geotoolbox).

The field is beatable with dated, sourced pages.

## AI Overviews
Google showed an AI Overview on 17 of the 21 searches checked. The sources it cites: the official site, Reddit, YouTube, NerdWallet and small blogs. Being cited needs the page indexed, a direct answer in the first lines, and a date. Our pages already follow that pattern.
